# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in OpenAPI Studio a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences, identities, and skill levels.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively.
- Assume good intent while addressing disagreements professionally.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus discussions on the work and technical subject matter.
- Respect different levels of experience and knowledge.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for new contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Threats or deliberately hostile behavior.
- Sexualized comments or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sustained unreasonable disruption of project discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where participants are representing or directly contributing to OpenAPI Studio.

Project maintainers may also apply these standards to behavior occurring outside project spaces when it directly affects the safety or participation of the OpenAPI Studio community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers.

Do not publicly disclose sensitive information about an incident.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to the process described in `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, responses to violations may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from project participation.
4. Permanent restriction from project participation.

Enforcement decisions will consider the circumstances, severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior.
